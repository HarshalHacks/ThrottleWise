I'm Harshal, a Class 10 student with a lifelong love of cars. ThrottleWise started with one frustration: when my family looked at buying a new car, the information was scattered across dozens of websites, often out of date, and almost never explained why one variant cost more than another.

So I set out to build the site I wished existed. One place where every variant of every car sold in India is laid out clearly, where you can see exactly what you gain when you step up a trim, and where every number can be traced back to a real source.

How ThrottleWise is built

  • Researched, not copied. Every price, engine figure, mileage claim and feature list is checked against manufacturer websites and cross-referenced with CarDekho, CarWale and Autocar India.
  • Honest about gaps. Where no reliable source confirms a figure, the site shows NA. It never fills a blank with a guess, and estimates are always labelled as estimates.
  • Built for decisions. The Find-My-Car quiz, Car Battle, variant comparison and the 50-city on-road price and EMI calculator exist to help you choose, not just to browse.
  • Free and independent. No dealer sponsorships, no paid placements. The rankings and comparisons are driven by the specs alone.

ThrottleWise is a living project and I update it as new cars launch and prices change. If you spot an error, have a suggestion, or just want to talk cars, I'd genuinely love to hear from you.
